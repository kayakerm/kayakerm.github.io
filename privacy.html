Privacy policy

Home Calendar Link is used only by the household that runs it, on its own Home Assistant server at home.

  • What is accessed: Google Calendar events of the signed-in account, so they can be shown and managed in Home Assistant.
  • Where it goes: calendar data is stored only on that household's own Home Assistant server. It is not sent to, sold to or shared with anyone else.
  • No tracking: no analytics, advertising or third-party services are used.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the integration from Home Assistant.
